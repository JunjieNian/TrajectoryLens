Reading notes

These are family-level aggregates, not individual trajectories. Families contain only correct runs and have at least three members.

Laplace smoothing uses α = 0.5. Unvisited ordinary rows still carry probability; EOS states are forced to self-loop.

Probability assigned to EOS_wrong therefore does not prove an observed incorrect trajectory in this correct-only family.

METHOD & FORMAT

Transparent alignment rules

This workspace inspects original records. The atlas uses exact identity alignment, without semantic embeddings, outcome propagation, or automatic trap detection.

  1. state / state_id: matching explicit identities align to the same node.
  2. keys: without an explicit identity, align by the complete sorted exact key set.
  3. Other records: align by normalized action/tool text, then normalized full text. The rules used appear after import.

Node positions aid reading and do not represent latent-space distance. A revisit is not proof of a mistake, trap, or wasted work. Jaccard measures identity overlap between state sets, not strategy equivalence.

Recommended minimal format

{
  "name": "My experiment",
  "runs": [{
    "id": "run-1", "model": "agent-a",
    "outcome": "success",
    "steps": [{
      "state": "inspect:file-a",
      "label": "Inspect file A",
      "text": "Original action + observation",
      "tokens": 240
    }]
  }]
}

Optional outcome: success / failure / unknown. Optional tokens and duration_ms must be finite, nonnegative numbers; missing costs remain unknown. Simple steps / trajectory / messages formats are also accepted, with matching notes after import.

Relationship to the papers

TraceGraph and SliceGraph research on process differences inspired this tool. It does not reproduce their mutual-kNN, BCC, activation representations, or family-discovery algorithms. Released examples directly read fixed-version SliceGraph files and preserve their provenance.
